OTEK Rental Management

Rent out equipment with serial-level tracking, QR labels, digital signatures, automatic late fees and one-click invoicing — all inside Odoo.

Everything a rental desk needs

One module covers the whole journey, from adding an item to the catalogue to invoicing the customer.

Equipment & categories

Organise your catalogue in unlimited category levels, with daily, weekly and monthly rates per item and live stock counts.

Serial-number tracking

Track every physical unit. Serials are generated automatically or in bulk, with a full status history for each one.

QR codes & labels

Every serial gets a branded QR code automatically, ready to print on labels. Add your company logo to the centre.

 

Full rental lifecycle

Draft, Reserved, Ongoing, Returned, Invoiced. Reserve equipment, hand it over, take it back and bill it.

Partial pickup & return

Customers rarely take or return everything at once. Hand over or receive just some serials without closing the project.

Signatures & damage

Capture the customer's signature on pickup and return, record damage with severity and repair cost, and keep photos on file.

 

Late fees & invoicing

Overdue rentals accrue late fees by fixed daily rate or percentage. Generate the customer invoice straight from the project.

Audit trail

Every status change, amount change and scan is logged in the chatter with who did it and when.

REST API

API-key-secured endpoints to look up serials, rent, return and list equipment or projects from external systems.

See it in action

Projects

See every rental at a glance

The kanban board groups projects by stage with the customer, dates, duration and total on every card. Switch to list or calendar view when you need a different angle.

✓  Overview of Draft, Reserved, Returned and Invoiced work
✓  Overdue and damage badges on the cards
✓  List and calendar views included
 
Catalogue

A catalogue that shows real availability

Equipment cards show what is available, what is out and the day rate, tagged by category, so staff can quote a customer without opening a single record.

✓  Available / total and rented counts per item
✓  Filter to active and available items
✓  Unlimited nested categories
 
Serial numbers

Every unit, tracked individually

Turn on serial tracking and generate serials automatically. Each unit carries its own status, current project and QR code.

✓  Auto-generate serials or create them in bulk
✓  Status per unit: available, reserved, rented, damaged
✓  Serials with history are protected from accidental deletion
 
Handover

Partial pickup with a signature

Tick exactly the serials the customer is taking today, collect their signature on screen and confirm. The rest of the booking stays reserved.

✓  Pick up or return any subset of a project
✓  On-screen customer signature
✓  Notes recorded with the handover
 
Late fees

Overdue rentals bill themselves

When a rental passes its end date the project is flagged overdue and the late fee is calculated for you, either from a daily rate or as a percentage of the rental amount.

✓  Red overdue ribbon and days-overdue counter
✓  Late fee added to the grand total automatically
✓  Configurable in Settings
 
Assignment

Assign exact serials to each line

Add equipment lines, choose how many, and let the module assign serials or pick them by hand. Totals and the chatter history update as you go.

✓  Auto-assign or select serials manually
✓  Unit price and subtotal per line
✓  Every amount and status change logged

Connect other systems

A small REST API, secured with an API key, lets kiosks, websites and third-party tools work with your rental data.

GET/api/rental/serial/<serial_number>Status of one serial
POST/api/rental/serial/rentQuick rent
POST/api/rental/serial/returnQuick return
GET/api/rental/equipment/listEquipment catalogue
GET/api/rental/equipment/<id>One equipment record
GET/api/rental/project/listRental projects
POST/api/rental/project/createCreate a project

Get started

Requirements

Odoo 19.0 (Community or Enterprise)
Apps: Sales, Inventory, Invoicing, Contacts
Python packages: qrcode, Pillow

Install

1. Install the Python packages: pip install qrcode[pil] Pillow
2. Place the module in your addons path and update the Apps list
3. Install OTEK Rental Management

Access levels

Rental User: create and edit projects, view equipment
Rental Manager: full access, deletion and settings
